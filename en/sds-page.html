Gel: stacking and resolving
▶

Stacking 4% (large pores, packs the sample into a narrow zone) + resolving 7.5–15%: dense (12–15%) for small proteins, loose (7.5–10%) for large ones.

Precast gels or self-cast
▶

Bio-Rad TGX (Tris-glycine, up to a year at 4 °C), Thermo NuPAGE/Bolt (Bis-Tris MES/MOPS, sharper bands, small proteins). Price 10–25 €.

Self-cast: acrylamide monomer is neurotoxic (in precast gels it is polymerized — nontoxic), APS+TEMED — polymerization starts immediately, ~2–3 € per gel. Daily series + a grant → self-cast; one-off experiments → precast.

Assembly and well rinsing
▶

Open the cassette, remove the tape from the bottom edge (otherwise no current!), assemble, fill with 1× running buffer: into the tank and the upper chamber.

Gels are stored in their own storage buffer (a preservative, NOT the running buffer). Wells are rinsed by pipetting up and down ABOVE the wells — the flow washes it out and replaces it with running buffer. No need to aspirate it separately: it disperses into the tank volume and is harmless there. If not removed — "torn" lanes at the start.

Loading
▶

Gel-loading tips (long and thin — a regular Eppendorf tip cannot reach into a deep narrow well; no filter needed — filters are for PCR), elbows on the bench, slowly. The sample with glycerol sinks to the well bottom. Molecular-weight marker — into the first well.

Run
▶

80 V ~10–15 min (packing in the stacking gel; the blue front compresses into a line) → 120–200 V 45–90 min (separation). Done: the dye front at the bottom edge.

Overheating → "smiling" lanes: mix the buffer thoroughly, do not push the voltage.

Tanks: "two schools"
▶

Bio-Rad Mini-PROTEAN Tetra or Thermo XCell SureLock — just two manufacturers, one principle, consumables not interchangeable (like Canon/Nikon).
