Running buffer (electrode)
▶

Purpose: carries the current, enables migration in SDS-PAGE.

10× stock per 1 L: Tris base 30.3 g + glycine 144 g + SDS 10 g + water; do not adjust pH (~8.3 by itself). Working: dilute 1:10.

Storage: 10× concentrate — months at RT (the manufacturer states 24 months; up to a year after opening — practice); do NOT freeze (SDS precipitates); yellowing over time is normal. Working 1× — ~1 month at RT, 2–3 months at 4 °C; the discipline — fresh every working week.

Transfer buffer
▶

Tris-glycine with 20% methanol: methanol helps protein binding to the membrane.

Working 1×: 50 mL 10× stock + 100 mL methanol + 350 mL water. Bis-Tris variant (no methanol): 25 mM Bicine, 25 mM Bis-Tris, 1 mM EDTA, pH 7.2 — keeps 6 months at 4 °C.

Storage: with methanol — PREPARE FRESH for every transfer (methanol is volatile, the composition drifts).

TBS-T (wash buffer)
▶

Per 1 L: Tris 6.1 g + NaCl 8.68 g + Tween-20 0.5 mL; pH 8.0 (HCl); water to 1 L. PBS-T — an equivalent alternative.

Storage: 4–25 °C, weeks; with sodium azide 0.02–0.05% — months at 4 °C, BUT azide must not meet HRP conjugates (inhibits peroxidase) or systems with metal parts (corrosion).

Loading buffer
▶

4×: SDS + reducing agent (fresh: DTT 200–400 mM in the stock = 100 mM in the sample, or β-mercaptoethanol) + glycerol + bromophenol blue + Tris-HCl pH 6.8. Mixing: 3 parts sample + 1 part 4×.

Storage: commercial −20 °C up to 12 months / 4 °C up to 6 months; homemade — base for months at RT + reducer on the day, or complete aliquots in the freezer. Details — Loading buffer stage.

Blocking buffer
▶

5% nonfat dry milk in TBS-T (routine) or 1–5% BSA (phospho-targets — milk forbidden: casein = a phosphoprotein).

Storage: ONLY fresh, on the day of use; milk is a bacterial growth medium; a day in the fridge is the limit.

Make-up buffer
▶

Not a separate reagent but a technique: equalizing the final lane volume. Options: diluted 1× loading buffer (cleanest), water (≤5–10 µL), lysis buffer (if the samples are in it). Details — Loading buffer stage.

Storage summary rule
▶

Concentrate — months at RT (do not freeze) → working 1× — weeks at RT / longer in the cold → everything with milk or methanol — on the day of use.

Questions from our walkthrough

❓ Running buffer: buy a ready concentrate or make it ourselves?

Both are legitimate. A commercial 10× (Bio-Rad, Thermo) gives reproducibility and a lot number for documentation, but costs ~40–70 €/L. Homemade is the lab norm: Tris base 30.3 g + glycine 144 g + SDS 10 g per 1 L of water, do not adjust pH (~8.3 by itself); the composition is identical, the price 10–20× lower. Practice: regular gel series — make it yourself (glycine dissolves slowly; SDS foams — do not vortex; do not freeze); commercial — for small volumes or QC traceability. Working 1× — fresh, dilute 1:10.
