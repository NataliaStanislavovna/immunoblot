Sample Preparation
covered ▶

Stage key points:

  • Lysis: buffer with SDS + protease inhibitors (EDTA or cocktail) + cold
  • BCA on an aliquot (not Bradford — SDS interferes; not after denaturation — DTT interferes)
  • Load calculation: 10–50 µg protein per lane, equalizing volumes
  • Denaturation: 95 °C 5 min, SDS + DTT/mercaptoethanol (break S–S bridges)

Loading Buffer & Volume Make-up
covered ▶

Stage key points:

  • Loading buffer 4×: SDS + reducing agent + glycerol + bromophenol blue + Tris
  • Mixing: 3 parts sample + 1 part 4× buffer
  • Heat 95 °C 5 min → ice → spin 10–30 s (condensate!)
  • Make-up buffer — equalizing the final lane volume

3
SDS-PAGE: Electrophoresis
in progress ▶

Stage key points:

  • Gel: 4% stacking + 7.5–15% resolving (density matched to target mass)
  • Precast gels (TGX, NuPAGE) are stored in their own buffer — flush the wells by pipetting
  • Loading: gel-loading tips, slowly; marker — into the first well
  • Run: 80 V (stacking) → 120–200 V (separation), ~1–1.5 h

Transfer to Membrane
not started ▶

Stage key points:

  • Wet tank: full tank, hours — heavy proteins
  • Semi-dry: 15–60 min — routine, medium proteins
  • Dry (Turbo): 7–15 min, proprietary cassettes
  • Membranes: nitrocellulose / PVDF (wet in methanol)

Blocking & Antibodies
not started ▶

Stage key points:

  • 5% milk or 1–5% BSA in TBS-T, 1 h at RT
  • Milk is forbidden for phospho-targets
  • Primary: 1:500–1:5000, overnight 4 °C = more specific
  • Secondary against the primary's host species: 1 h RT
