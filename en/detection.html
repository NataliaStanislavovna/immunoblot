Chemiluminescence (ECL)
▶

HRP + luminol/peroxide → photons. Film (old school, narrow linear range) or a CCD camera. Maximum sensitivity.

Fluorescent and NIR
▶

Secondaries with fluorophores (Alexa, IRDye). NIR (Li-Cor Odyssey, 700/800 nm): wide linear range, "target + loading control" multiplex on one membrane — the current standard of quantitative Western blotting.

Chromogenic
▶

HRP + DAB → brown staining. No instruments, for a quick yes/no.

Normalization
▶

Housekeeping proteins (actin, GAPDH — they change themselves!) vs total protein (stain-free, the modern standard).
