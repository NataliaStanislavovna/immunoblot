Simple Western
▶

ProteinSimple/Bio-Techne (Jess, Abby, Leo): separation in a CE-SDS capillary + immunodetection in the same capillary. No gels, membranes, or transfer. 13–25 samples, ~3 h hands-off, picogram sensitivity, RePlex (re-probing).

In-Cell Western
▶

Fixed cells in a 96-well plate + antibodies, NIR detection — quantitative screening (RNAi, drugs).

Automation and analysis
▶

BlotCycler, EUROBlotOne — automated wash stations. Analysis: Image Studio, EurolineSCAN — remove reading subjectivity.
